# Real Bay Area ghost kitchens — research notes

Not wired into the app. The live catalogue is ported from the operator's own hub
(goodneighborbuilder.com). This file is the shortlist of *genuine* local
ghost-kitchen brands, kept because several of the ported entries are clearly
placeholder and will need replacing.

Both brands named directly by the client were confirmed real.

## Worth knowing

- **Lotus & Lime is the closest match to this product.** It runs the same brand
  out of three separate CloudKitchens units and is categorised on Yelp as a
  food-delivery service, not a restaurant.
- **The Lumpia Company** publishes real prices on its own site — $12–15 across
  lumpia, wings and rice plates. The most reliable source found.
- **Pamilya converted to a storefront in mid-2024**, so it is no longer a ghost
  kitchen. Include only if the client wants brick-and-mortar too.
- Delivery platforms (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, Yelp business pages, Toast)
  all block automated fetch. Every real price above came from a brand-owned
  site, allmenus, SinglePlatform, CaterCow or Zerocater.

## Searched, not usable

- **Halo Lumpia Kitchen** (Oakland) — real lumpia ghost kitchen, shows CLOSED as
  of May 2026.
- **Aria Korean Street Food** — was a DoorDash Kitchens tenant; San Jose listing
  now closed.
- **Adobolicious** (Fremont), **Tapsilog Express** (San Leandro) — real Filipino
  businesses but conventional dine-in, not ghost kitchens.
- No public tenant roster exists for the CloudKitchens Hayward facility, so its
  other virtual brands could not be enumerated.

## Before using any of these

These are other people's businesses. Putting their name, menu and prices in a
live ordering app needs their agreement — the research here is for deciding who
to approach, not a licence to list them.
