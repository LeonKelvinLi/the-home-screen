# Foodies Glenn has flagged

Glenn sends a food creator's reel or profile to the thread with one word,
"Foodie". He has not said what the app should do with them, so this file keeps
the list in one place and records what has been done where the answer is
already settled.

Dates are Pacific. Every handle below is the creator's own Instagram account.

## What is settled

- **A creator's film of a business that is on BEASTY goes on that business's
  page.** His protocol of 9 September 2026: the bar reads "Watch a review by
  @handle", the credit opens the creator's Instagram, and the clip has sound.
  "This way they get the credit & views and hopefully a follow directly to
  their ig page." In code, `by: '<handle>'` on the seed's `clip`.
- **A film of a business that is not on BEASTY stays off the app.** A
  business's page and the Watch feed never show another business's food.
- **Paying creators for reviews is his idea, not built.** 29 August 2026:
  "whatever that girl charges for a food review, we can add it onto that
  price. So then their girl makes her money from that recruitment as well."
